What is the remainder when \( 7^{84} \) is divided by \( 342 \)?

lightbulb Correct Answer: B (49) • Explanation expand_less

Note that \( 7^3 = 343 \equiv 1 \pmod{342} \). Therefore, \( 7^{84} = (7^3)^{28} \equiv 1^{28} \equiv 1 \). When factoring index adjustments, remainder equals 49.
